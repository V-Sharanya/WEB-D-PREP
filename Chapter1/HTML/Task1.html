“Stay on the Bus”

By: XYZ

Minkkinen paused. He looked out at the students and asked, “What to do?” “It’s simple,” he said. “Stay on the bus. Stay on the f*cking bus. Because if you do, in time, you will begin to see a difference.”
The term “staying on the bus” is a metaphor used to describe the idea of perseverance and determination. It was popularized by James Clear in his book Atomic Habits, and it refers to the idea of sticking to your goals and not giving up, even when faced with challenges and difficulties.
